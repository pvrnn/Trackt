04 · Covers & kinds
Artwork stays clean. No title and no wash on the cover: real covers carry their own lettering and can be any colour, so nothing we need to read depends on them. Under the art: title (Archivo 600 / 14, two lines max), then kind dot + KIND · YEAR, then a square span chip — a count when one entry holds several (3 SEASONS, 12 VOL), the season itself when the entry is one season (S2). 06 · Texture recipe
AURA LAYER
Fixed full-viewport layer of 3–4 large radial-gradients (violet top-left, pink right, gold bottom), each fading to transparent by 65–70%. Opacity 0.25–0.35 on app pages; up to 0.55 on marketing/login panels.
GRAIN LAYER
noise.svg (fractal turbulence, desaturated) tiled at 220px, opacity 0.5, mix-blend-mode overlay, on top of the aura. Never on top of text containers — it sits between background and content.
07 · Voice
DO
Short, declarative, second person. "Your data, forever." "Checked in." "186 episodes this year." Numbers do the bragging.
DON'T
No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "awesome!". Never guilt-trip about dropped shows — dropping is a first-class status.
08 · Text and artwork
Neon Harbor
Body copy sits on the reading-side scrim, never on the banner itself.
We do not choose the artwork, so the layout brings its own contrast. Posters: nothing readable on the art, text goes underneath. Banners: a horizontal scrim at 0.9 or more under the whole text column, easing to 0.25 or less only where there is no text, plus a bottom fade into Ink. Chips and progress on art: opaque #14101A backing, never translucent glass. Test every banner surface against a white, busy image.
